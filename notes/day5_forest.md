# 🌲 Day 5: Random Forest (Ensemble Learning)

### Q1: What is an "Ensemble" model?
**A:** It is a model that combines multiple individual models to get a better result. "Many heads are better than one!"

### Q2: How does a Random Forest vote?
**A:** Each tree in the forest makes a prediction (e.g., "High Profit"). The forest looks at all 100 predictions and chooses the one that got the most votes.

### Q3: What is "Feature Importance"?
**A:** It is a technique that assigns a score to input features based on how useful they were at predicting a target variable. This is great for business because it tells you exactly what drives your profit!

### Q4: Why use Random Forest over a single Decision Tree?
**A:** A single tree is very "sensitive" and can easily overfit (memorize). A Random Forest is "stable" and "generalizes" better to new, unseen data.

### 🧠 Tutor Challenge:
**Scenario:** You have 10 friends. 1 friend is a genius but sometimes gets confused. The other 9 are average.
**Question:** If you want to win a trivia contest, would you rather have just the genius on your team, or all 10 friends voting together?
**Answer:** All 10 friends! This is the core of Random Forest. Even if the genius makes a mistake, the group will likely correct it.
