# 🏅 Day 7: The Grand Finale (Model Selection)

### Q1: What is a "Model Competition"?
**A:** It is the process of training multiple types of AI on the same data to see which one performs best. In the real world, we rarely know which AI is best until we test them against each other.

### Q2: Why is RMSE better than MSE for humans?
**A:** MSE is measured in "Units Squared" (which makes no sense to us). RMSE is measured in the *original units* (like Dollars). If RMSE is $400, we know exactly what that means for our bank account!

### Q3: What is "Inference"?
**A:** Inference is when you use your trained "Brain" to guess new data. We did this in the **Final Business Report** when we guessed the revenue for a hypothetical 50-unit sale.

### Q4: What are the 3 pillars of Machine Learning you learned?
**A:** 
1. **Data Prep** (Wrangling)
2. **Model Training** (Learning)
3. **Model Evaluation** (Testing)
